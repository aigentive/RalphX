# RalphX.app v0.84.0

RalphX.app 0.84.0 surfaces base-update repair progress, continues eligible repairs through publishing, and hardens recovery of maintenance workflows.

## User-Facing Changes
- **Base updates and repairs** now show a persistent, stage-by-stage maintenance operation instead of a frozen generic publish state, including Updating base, Repairing, Validating, Reviewing, and Publishing. [7c983479](https://github.com/aigentive/ralphx.app/commit/7c983479b8b983b74cea07510ccf92f6c971dd7b)
- **Repair continuation** follows the original workflow automatically when eligible, including required Workspace Review and Auto Publish; with Auto Publish off, RalphX.app stops at **“Base updated — ready to publish”** with one publish action. [7c983479](https://github.com/aigentive/ralphx.app/commit/7c983479b8b983b74cea07510ccf92f6c971dd7b)
- **Publish controls** now reflect the active maintenance operation: competing Commit & Publish actions are disabled during automatic stages, while blocked operations show their concrete reason and a single retry path. [7c983479](https://github.com/aigentive/ralphx.app/commit/7c983479b8b983b74cea07510ccf92f6c971dd7b)
- **Publication status** again scopes receipts and event history to the active publish attempt, including specific recovery messaging when PR description publication fails. [7c983479](https://github.com/aigentive/ralphx.app/commit/7c983479b8b983b74cea07510ccf92f6c971dd7b)

## Fixes And Polish
- **Late or superseded repair completions** no longer surface as stale-attempt failures; RalphX.app keeps the current operation visible and reconciles it safely. [7c983479](https://github.com/aigentive/ralphx.app/commit/7c983479b8b983b74cea07510ccf92f6c971dd7b)
- **Maintenance toasts** now report failed workspace verification as unverified rather than incorrectly claiming that an operation completed. [7c983479](https://github.com/aigentive/ralphx.app/commit/7c983479b8b983b74cea07510ccf92f6c971dd7b)

## Developer And Maintainer Changes
- Introduces durable maintenance-operation state, attempt fencing, and external-effect checkpoints so repair, publish, and recovery paths share one authoritative workflow. [7c983479](https://github.com/aigentive/ralphx.app/commit/7c983479b8b983b74cea07510ccf92f6c971dd7b)
- Hardens repair persistence and authority checks by rejecting settled or cross-conversation attempts and failing closed on malformed persisted state. [7c983479](https://github.com/aigentive/ralphx.app/commit/7c983479b8b983b74cea07510ccf92f6c971dd7b)
- Restores repair-flow CI invariants, integration coverage, and visual snapshot coverage. [7c983479](https://github.com/aigentive/ralphx.app/commit/7c983479b8b983b74cea07510ccf92f6c971dd7b)
